Who We Are

Building character.
Building community.

Youth
Driven • Led • Owned

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group is a registered community-based organisation rooted in Changara, Teso North. We exist to nurture young Muslims who are grounded in faith, equipped with knowledge, and capable of economic independence.

Through seminars, madrasa support, Ramadan outreach, savings schemes and inter-mosque coordination, we raise a generation that serves both the Ummah and the wider Kenyan society.

Islamic Values

Dawah, moral formation and authentic knowledge.

Economic Power

What We Do

Core Programmes

Practical initiatives that strengthen faith, skills and community welfare across Teso North.

Ramadan & Eid Iftar

Community meals and food baskets for families, orphans and elders throughout the holy month.

Eid al-Adha Qurbani

Coordinated sacrifice and distribution of meat to vulnerable households in Changara and surrounding villages.

December Youth Seminar

Our flagship multi-day gathering of scholars, workshops and fellowship for youth from across the sub-county.

Savings & Enterprise

Structured table-banking that helps young people launch small businesses and farming projects.

Madrasa & Competitions

Support for local madrasas plus annual Quran and Hadith contests with awards for students.

Inter-Mosque Network

Active coordination between mosque youth groups and committees across Busia County.
