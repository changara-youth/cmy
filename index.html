Who We Are

Building Strong Islamic Character & Community Self-Reliance

Changara Muslim Youth Self Help Group is a registered community-based youth initiative based in Changara, Teso North Sub-County, Busia County. Founded on Islamic brotherhood, moral responsibility and social welfare, we connect young Muslims to build a vibrant, empowered community.

From our flagship Annual December Youth Seminars and Madrasa competitions to Ramadan Iftar distribution and youth savings schemes, we nurture righteous leaders who serve the Ummah and the wider society in Kenya.

Islamic Values & Dawah

Strengthening faith, morals and sound Islamic knowledge.

Economic Empowerment

Table banking, entrepreneurship and skill development.

Community Welfare

Ramadan & Eid support, orphan care and social assistance.

Inter-Mosque Unity

Uniting youth across mosques in Teso North & Busia.
